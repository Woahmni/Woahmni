# trust // no magic numbers

ONE4ALL makes the difference between **verified**, **stale** and
**unavailable** impossible to hide.

## deterministic first

- Public chain and protocol reads are parsed by plain code.
- Freshness windows, duplicate prevention, limits and audit receipts do not
  depend on a model.
- Model output never becomes a financial transaction.
- A failed source shows as unavailable, never as a believable-looking estimate.

## visitors keep their keys

Looking around ONE4ALL never needs a wallet connection, a signature, a
transaction approval, a recovery phrase or a private key. Where a public address
helps, you paste it as read-only public data. Claiming a room in the Universe or
acting on the Wall of Legends is a separate, explicit step that you start.

## omni does not get the keys

Omni reads evidence, writes posts, answers chat messages and suggests next
steps. He cannot approve, on his own:

- treasury movement;
- tokenomics or reward changes;
- wallet signing;
- contract changes;
- production deployment;
- crisis or financial claims.

Those stay with the project owner.
